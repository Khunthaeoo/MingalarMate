Project Identity & Mission

About MingalarMate

An independent, student-led guide created to welcome, inform, and empower Myanmar and international students throughout their educational adventure in Taiwan.

📅 Last reviewed: November 2026 • Project Phase: Version 1 (Core Information Architecture)

The Story Behind Our Name

In Myanmar, "Mingalarba" (မင်္ဂလာပါ) is the traditional greeting meaning "auspicious blessings to you". It carries warmth, respect, and deep hospitality.

Combined with "Mate", representing a helpful peer and companion, MingalarMate signifies a warm, trustworthy companion walking alongside students as they adapt to life, academics, and culture in Taiwan.

Navigating university admissions, legal work permissions, housing, and health insurance across different languages can be intimidating. MingalarMate bridges this information gap by synthesizing complex regulations into organized, friendly, and actionable insights.

Our Core Motto

"Clear guidance, verified resources, and a warm welcome for every international student in Taiwan."

Who We Help

While MingalarMate was initiated with a special focus on the needs of Myanmar students, our resources are structured to support all prospective and current international scholars in Taiwan:

Prospective Students

Individuals researching Taiwan higher education, comparing university departments, preparing document authentication, and seeking scholarship opportunities.

Incoming New Arrivals

Students preparing for departure or newly landed in Taiwan who need step-by-step guidance on Alien Resident Certificates (ARC), SIM cards, dorms, and bank accounts.

Enrolled Scholars

Current students seeking verified knowledge on Ministry of Labor work permits, National Health Insurance (NHI) benefits, and safe student living practices.

Our Principles: Independence & Accuracy

🏛️

Independent Status Disclaimer

MingalarMate is strictly an independent student information project. We are not an official arm of the Taiwan government, not affiliated with the Taiwan Ministry of Education, not an employment agency, and not an immigration consultancy.

We do not invent rules, fees, deadlines, or requirements. Whenever specific official procedures are discussed, we direct readers to official portals such as the National Immigration Agency, the Ministry of Labor, and accredited universities.

Current Scope & Future Vision

We are committed to purposeful, sustainable development. Here is where we stand today and where we are heading:

Version 1: The Static Foundation (Current)

  • Responsive 5-page static architecture published via GitHub Pages.
  • English-first foundational content with zero external frameworks or tracking scripts.
  • Verified links to official government and university authorities.
  • Lightweight, accessible, and fast on both mobile and desktop browsers.

Future Roadmap (Phase 2 & Beyond)

  • Multilingual Support: Full translations into Burmese (မြန်မာဘာသာ) and Traditional Chinese (繁體中文).
  • Student Stories & Mentorship: Experiences and practical wisdom shared by senior Myanmar graduates in Taiwan.
  • Campus Directory: Curated contacts for university international offices and student associations.
  • Interactive Checklists: Offline-capable pre-departure and arrival checklist helpers.

Community Feedback

Information changes as government policies and university guidelines evolve. If you notice an outdated link, a changed policy, or have suggestions for topics you would like to see covered, we welcome feedback from students, alumni, and educators.

Contributions, corrections, and issue reports can be submitted via our public repository on GitHub.

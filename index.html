A Clear 4-Step Student Journey

Step 1: Explore & Apply

Select your academic field and university via official portals like Study in Taiwan. Prepare certified academic records, English or Chinese language test scores, and apply for government or institutional scholarships.

Step 2: Visa & Pre-Departure

Once you receive an official admission letter, apply for your Resident Visa through your nearest Taipei Economic and Cultural Office (TECO). Prepare your health exam report and emergency funds.

Step 3: Arrival & Settlement

Apply for your Alien Resident Certificate (ARC) at the National Immigration Agency within statutory deadlines. Set up your local SIM card, student bank account, and campus dormitory or apartment.

Step 4: Health Insurance & Work Permits

Enroll in Taiwan’s National Health Insurance (NHI) system once eligible. If you plan to work part-time, secure an official student Work Permit before beginning any paid activity.
